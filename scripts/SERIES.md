# Series plan: One object, one supply chain

Concept: one everyday object per Short, traced from raw material to the shelf in under 60 seconds. Generic products only, no brands. Flat, non-realistic animation. Every claim sourced in the episode file.

To avoid YouTube's "template-only" monetization exclusion, each episode uses a different storytelling structure, not only a different product.

| # | Object | Structure | Hook idea | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Plastic water bottle | Forward chain: raw material → product → shelf, with a mid-video twist | "This bottle didn't start as a bottle." | Rendered |
| 2 | Cotton T-shirt | Reverse chain: start at the shelf and rewind to the field | Start on the price tag, then rewind | Idea; research needed |
| 3 | Pencil | Parts count: split the object into parts, each with its own origin | "A pencil is at least 4 different industries." | Idea; research needed |
| 4 | Banana | Clock: a countdown from harvest to shelf, showing elapsed days | "This banana was picked weeks before you saw it." | Idea; research needed |

Every hook above is a working hypothesis and must be sourced before scripting. Drop or reword any hook the sources don't support.
