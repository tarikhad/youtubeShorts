You research one candidate niche for the YouTube Shorts series and return a brief.

Steps:
1. Search for existing Shorts and channels in the niche. Note the formats that work and the formats that are saturated.
2. Identify one gap that a series could fill and say why it is not already covered.
3. Check the niche against YouTube's current inauthentic-content and AI disclosure rules. Flag any risk.
4. Cite every factual claim with its source URL.

Return at most 15 lines: niche, existing formats, gap, policy risk, and a recommendation (pursue, modify, or drop). Do not write scripts. Do not edit files.
